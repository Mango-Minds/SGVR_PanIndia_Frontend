# App Store Listing Content Guide

This guide provides templates and guidelines for filling out your App Store Connect listing fields for "In Bharat" app.

## Character Limits

- **Promotional Text**: 170 characters (can be updated without app review)
- **Description**: 4,000 characters (full app description)
- **Keywords**: 100 characters (comma-separated, no spaces after commas)

---

## 1. Promotional Text (170 characters max)

**Purpose**: Appears above your app description. Can be updated anytime without submitting a new version.

**Best Practices**:
- Highlight new features, promotions, or special offers
- Use compelling, action-oriented language
- Update frequently to keep content fresh
- Don't repeat your app name

### Template Options:

## 2. Description (4,000 characters max)

**Purpose**: Full description of your app. This is what users read to understand your app.

**Best Practices**:
- Start with a compelling hook (first 2-3 lines are most important)
- Use bullet points for features
- Include benefits, not just features
- Use line breaks for readability
- End with a call to action

## 3. Keywords (100 characters max)

**Purpose**: Help users find your app through App Store search. Comma-separated, no spaces after commas.

**Best Practices**:
- Use relevant, searchable terms
- Include synonyms and variations
- Don't repeat your app name
- Use common misspellings if relevant
- Focus on what users search for
- No spaces after commas

## Tips for Optimization

1. **A/B Testing**: Try different promotional texts to see what works best
2. **Localization**: Consider translating content for different markets
3. **Screenshots**: Use screenshots that show key features
4. **App Preview Video**: Consider adding a video (optional but recommended)
5. **Regular Updates**: Update promotional text regularly to highlight new features
6. **Keywords Research**: Use App Store Connect analytics to refine keywords
